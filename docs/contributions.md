# My contribution and team work

## Team

Phoebe Chen, Daniel Overdeck, Ben Saperstein, and Aric Wang.

Our team placed **second among nine teams** in the DSAC 2026 Datathon.

## My contribution

I handled the restaurant-level OLS regression, including interpretation of its reported performance. I created visualizations, particularly regression plots, and contributed substantially to making and delivering the presentation.

I also worked with the team on interpreting the wider findings and turning them into restaurant recommendations.

## Team methods

A teammate handled most of the sentiment-analysis implementation. The NLTK, Word2Vec, VADER, and gradient-boosting methods are included to describe the full team project, not to claim I independently implemented them.

This repository does not assign every code cell or chart to a named teammate without supporting evidence.

## Assistance and original work

The competition explicitly allowed AI tools and external resources. That permission does not establish the exact assistance used for every part of our original work; this repository does not invent a detailed historical AI-use log.

The retrospective portfolio text was assembled with AI assistance from our presentation, the problem statement, and documented contribution notes. No source analysis was changed or rerun.
